# Illustrative filling animation

[Watch or download the MP4 video](Aguamiel_Extractor_Filling_Animation_1080p.mp4).

The video shows the upright extractor, a transparent reference container with a geometric capacity of 10 L, and an animated liquid-level surface. Static blue arrows indicate flow direction. The housing is hidden to show the interior.

A prescribed constant flow of 5 L/min gives a nominal filling time of 120 s. Playback is accelerated. This is an illustrative CAD animation, not CFD or experimental flow validation. It does not calculate particle trajectories, turbulence, transient pressure, or the pump operating point. The reference container does not reproduce measured dimensions of the physical container.

Video: MP4 / H.264, 1920 x 1080 pixels, approximately 30 seconds, no audio.
